7. Best Practices Summary

✓ Do

  • Use colorblind-friendly palettes
  • Ensure sufficient contrast (4.5:1 minimum)
  • Use sequential colors for ordered data
  • Limit palette to 5-7 distinct colors
  • Test on different displays
  • Consider cultural color associations

✗ Don't

  • Don't rely on color alone to convey information
  • Don't use rainbow colors for unordered data
  • Don't use red-green for critical distinctions
  • Don't use too many similar shades
  • Don't ignore contrast ratios
  • Don't use neon or oversaturated colors
